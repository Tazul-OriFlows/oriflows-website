// QUESTIONS

Before you book the call

What footage do you need from me? +
Your raw footage, any shot notes or must-have moments, and a reference to your existing edit style — we match it, not replace it.
How long does a typical edit take? +
Turnaround depends on package and footage volume, but timelines are agreed upfront and tracked, so you always know when to expect delivery.
Will the edit match my studio's style? +
Yes. We start from your existing work as a style reference and go through a revision round to make sure it fits your brand before final delivery.
Is there a contract? +
No lock-in contract. You can send one wedding or a season's worth, and stop any time.
How do I send footage and get files back? +
Through a simple upload link and project tracker — no new complicated software to learn on your end.
