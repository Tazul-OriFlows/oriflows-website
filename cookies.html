Privacy Policy / Terms & Conditions / Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how OriFlows ("OriFlows", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on oriflows.com (the "Website"), what each category of cookie is used for, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal data more generally.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to give site owners information about how a website is used. Some cookies are set by the website you're visiting ("first-party cookies"), and others are set by a different organisation whose service is embedded on the page, such as a booking calendar ("third-party cookies").

2. Categories of cookies

Cookies are generally grouped into the following categories. We explain below which of these are currently active on the Website.

CategoryPurpose
Strictly necessaryRequired for the Website and its core features (such as the booking calendar) to function. These cannot be switched off without breaking that functionality.
Performance & analyticsHelp us understand how visitors use the Website — e.g. which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and where visitors come from — so we can improve it.
MarketingUsed to track visitors across websites in order to display relevant adverts, build retargeting audiences, or measure the effectiveness of ad campaigns.
FunctionalRemember choices you make (such as form field values already entered) to provide a more personalised experience.

3. Cookies currently used on this Website

OriFlows keeps cookie use to the minimum needed to run the Website. At present:

Strictly necessary Cal.com booking calendar When you use the "Pick a time" booking calendar embedded on our booking page, Cal.com (our scheduling provider) may set cookies required to display available time slots and confirm your booking. Without these, the booking calendar cannot function.
Strictly necessary Formspree form handling Our audit-request and contact forms are processed via Formspree. Formspree may set a cookie strictly necessary to process your form submission securely and prevent spam/abuse.

Performance, analytics and marketing cookies: the Website does not currently run third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics), advertising pixels, or marketing/retargeting cookies. If we introduce any of these in the future — for example to measure site performance or run ad campaigns — this Cookie Policy will be updated in advance, the table above will be revised to reflect the change, and, where required by UK law, a cookie consent banner will be added to obtain your permission before any non-essential cookie is set.

4. Your consent

Under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR, strictly necessary cookies (like those above, needed to deliver a service you've actively requested, such as loading the booking calendar) can be used without prior consent. Any performance, analytics, or marketing cookies we add in future will only be set after you have given your consent via a cookie banner, and you will be able to withdraw that consent at any time.

5. How to manage or disable cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block cookies from specific sites, or all sites, by default
  • Block third-party cookies specifically
  • Clear all cookies when you close your browser

Because browser settings differ, please check your browser's help menu for exact instructions — commonly under "Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies" (or equivalent). Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies, such as those used by our booking calendar, may prevent parts of the Website (like scheduling an audit call) from working correctly.

6. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. As there is no common industry standard for how sites should respond to DNT signals, the Website does not currently respond differently based on this setting — though, as noted above, we do not run analytics or marketing cookies at present regardless.
