Most UK dental practices lose patients quietly, not dramatically

A dental practice rarely loses a patient in one obvious moment. It happens in small gaps: a call that rings out during a busy Monday morning, a reminder that never went out, a treatment plan a patient agreed to verbally and then never followed up on. None of this shows up as a single crisis — it shows up as a slowly declining new-patient number and a recall list nobody has time to work through.

Dental clinic automation closes those specific gaps. It isn't a new phone system or a replacement for your team — it's a layer that catches what would otherwise be missed, and hands it back to your team as a booked appointment or a flagged follow-up.

How dental clinic automation works

OriFlows connects to the systems your practice already runs — your phone number, your calendar (Google Calendar or similar), and your patient communication channel — using GoHighLevel as the automation layer and n8n for the underlying workflow logic. Nothing about how a patient reaches you changes. What happens in the seconds and days after they reach you does.

Missed-call text-back

An unanswered call triggers an instant SMS with a booking link, so the patient hears from you within seconds instead of calling the next practice.

Appointment reminders

Multi-step SMS and email reminders before every appointment, with one-tap confirm or reschedule.

Lead and recall follow-up

New enquiries and overdue recall patients get automatic, personalised follow-up sequences until they book.

AI front desk (optional)

Covers after-hours and overflow calls, checking availability and booking directly into your calendar.

Specific use cases for private dental practices

Automation is only useful when it maps to a real, specific moment in your practice's day. Examples we build against most often:

  • A call comes in while reception is checking in a patient — the automation catches it and texts back within seconds.
  • A patient books online at 9pm — the automation confirms and adds it to the calendar without anyone at the practice touching it.
  • A hygiene recall is six weeks overdue — a follow-up sequence starts automatically instead of sitting on a spreadsheet.
  • An appointment is 24 hours away — a reminder goes out with a one-tap confirm or reschedule link, reducing the chance of an empty chair.

Integration and implementation

The system is built on GoHighLevel as the core CRM and communication layer, with n8n handling workflow automation and Groq-powered AI for personalised message content. It connects to your existing phone number (via Twilio), Google Calendar or Outlook, and your patient enquiry channels. Setup is handled end-to-end — you don't need to touch the backend.

Who this is suitable for

Frequently asked questions

Will this replace my front desk team? +
No. It's built to catch what your team physically can't — calls during lunch, after-hours enquiries, and the follow-up that falls through the cracks when reception is busy with patients in the chair. Your team stays in charge; the automation handles the gaps around them.
Do I need new software or a new phone number? +
No. The automation connects to your existing phone number, calendar and inbox. Patients keep calling the same number and booking the same way — what happens behind that number is what changes.
How long does it take to go live? +
Typically 7–14 days from your audit call: 2–5 days to map the system to your practice, then testing before it touches a real patient.
Is my patient data secure? +
Yes. Data is processed through GoHighLevel, which has its own data protection safeguards, and is only used to run your automations. A Data Processing Agreement is available on request.
What if it doesn't suit my practice? +
There's no lock-in contract. The free audit call is there specifically to establish whether automation makes sense for your call volume and patient value before you commit to anything.
